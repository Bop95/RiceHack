# Weather Insights & FinalFlow Mobility Connections

## Key Findings
1. **Heat Risk:** 56.1% of historical summer days (June–July) reach or exceed 30 degrees Celcious (or 86 degrees Fahrenheit)
   * **FinalFlow Action:** Requires establishing water refilling stations, shaded queueing structures, and medical tents at Meadowlands Station and Secaucus transfer hubs.
2. **Rain & Visibility Risk:** 38.0% of summer days experience rainfall, with 4.3% featuring low visibility (<= 5km or <= 3.11 miles)
   * **FinalFlow Action:** Rain and reduced visibility decrease walking speeds and vehicle throughput, justifying extra shuttle staging and covered transfer walkways.
