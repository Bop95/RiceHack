FinalFlow Weather Analytics: Figure Documentation
This document provides detailed descriptions, purpose, and operational insights for the visualizations generated by the FinalFlow weather processing pipeline.

**Figure 1: Cleaned Precipitation Distribution**
File: reports/figures/fig1_precipitation_quality.png

1. Data Source & Cleaning Logic
Dataset: data/processed/daily_weather_clean.csv

Key Transformation: Raw precipitation data was sanitized by removing technical sentinel values (e.g., -1 which indicated "No Rain"). Raw integer hundredths of a millimeter were converted to physical millimeters (mm) by dividing by 100.

2. Visualization Observations
Logarithmic Scale: The Y-axis uses a logarithmic scale to clearly visualize the rare but critical heavy rainfall events that would otherwise be masked by the high frequency of light rain days.

Heavy Rain Threshold: A dashed red line highlights the 20 mm threshold. Historical data identifies 394 such extreme events within the sample.

3. Purpose & Operational Insight
This graph serves as Pipeline Validation. It proves that the raw weather data was successfully sanitized. Operationally, it justifies the inclusion of rain-induced travel delays in the FinalFlow simulation engine, demonstrating that heavy rain is a frequent enough occurrence to warrant dedicated operational staging buffers.

**Figure 2: World Cup Window Weather Risk Frequency**
File: reports/figures/fig2_summer_risk_days.png

1. Data Source & Cleaning Logic
Dataset: data/summaries/weather_risk_days.csv

Sampling: This chart strictly analyzes historical observations (N = 7,698 days) within the actual tournament window (June–July).

2. Visualization Observations
The breakdown of historical days crossing key FinalFlow stress thresholds is:

Hot Days (30°C / 86°F or higher): 56.1% (4,318 days)

Rainy Days (greater than 0 mm): 38.0% (2,924 days)

Heavy Rain Days (greater than 20 mm): 5.1% (394 days)

Low Visibility Days (5 km or less): 4.3% (333 days)

3. Purpose & Operational Insight
This graph provides the Empirical Baseline Vulnerability for the tournament. It proves to stakeholders that extreme heat impacts over half of the summer days, while rain occurs on nearly two out of five days. Operationally, it provides mandatory directives for physical infrastructure: shaded queues, outdoor hydration points, and emergency shuttle staging are essential requirements, not optional additions.

**Figure 3: Summer Heat & Humidity Stress Matrix**
File: reports/figures/fig3_heat_humidity_matrix.png

1. Data Source & Cleaning Logic
Dataset: data/processed/daily_weather_clean.csv (Filtered for June and July).

Parameters: Correlation mapping between Maximum Temperature (°C) and Relative Humidity (%).

2. Visualization Observations
This chart uses a hexagonal binning density plot (Hexbin). The darkest orange/red hexes represent the highest density of historical observations.

Core Concentration: The highest density occurs between 28°C to 34°C matched with 60% to 80% humidity.

Threshold Reference: The dashed red vertical line indicates the 30°C heat warning threshold.

3. Purpose & Operational Insight
This matrix provides a Spectator Thermal Comfort Risk Assessment. It visualizes the compounding effect of humidity on high temperatures (the Heat Index). Because high humidity stifles evaporative cooling from sweat, it significantly elevates the risk of spectator heat exhaustion during long outdoor waits. Operationally, this informs FinalFlow's queue management algorithms to trigger cooling fans and priority boarding protocols when conditions forecast in the upper-right quadrant.

**Figure 4: FinalFlow Operational Risk Classification Distribution**
File: reports/figures/fig4_risk_levels_breakdown.png

1. Data Source & Cleaning Logic
Dataset: data/summaries/finalflow_weather_integration.csv

Classification Criteria: A weighted composite score was generated:
Score = 0.35 * Hot + 0.30 * Rain + 0.15 * Wind + 0.20 * Low_Visibility

2. Visualization Observations
The resulting distribution of classified days is:

Low Risk (Score under 0.3): 48.8% (22,377 days) — Normal operations.

Medium Risk (Score between 0.3 and 0.6): 44.9% (20,568 days) — Triggers covered queues and expanded shuttle capacity.

High Risk (Score 0.6 or higher): 6.3% (2,869 days) — Triggers water points, shade, slower speed assumptions, and active staff guidance.

3. Purpose & Operational Insight
This graph demonstrates the final System Integration. It shows how continuous weather variables are converted into discrete operational policy modes. This classification serves as the core "data dictionary" for the tournament chatbot and dashboard, enabling instant retrieval of specific operational recommendations based on forecast inputs.
