# Milestone August 3: Scoring Matrix Logic & Scenarios
**Author:** Đức Anh (Data Analyst) & Phương Anh (Business Analyst)

## 1. 4-Quadrant Opportunity-Risk Matrix Definition
- **High Opportunity + Low Risk:** Recommended Placement
- **High Opportunity + High Risk:** Avoided (Causes chokepoints)
- **Moderate Opportunity + Medium Risk:** Controlled Placement

## 2. Five Preliminary Findings
1. Vendor placements directly outside stadium gates create dangerous egress chokepoints.
2. Staggering merchandise locations 400-800m away absorbs post-match crowds effectively.
3. High precipitation drives traffic to covered/indoor adjacent zones.
4. Urban heat index is significantly higher on asphalt parking zones, requiring shaded canopies.
5. Transit corridors require controlled queuing lanes to preserve emergency access.
