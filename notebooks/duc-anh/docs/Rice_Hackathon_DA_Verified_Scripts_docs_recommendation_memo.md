# Milestone August 4: Executive Recommendation Memo
**Author:** Đức Anh (Data Analyst) & Phương Anh (Business Analyst)

## Executive Summary
To achieve "Commerce Without Congestion" during World Cup 2026 at MetLife Stadium, we propose a dynamic vendor placement strategy based on geospatial density and historical foot traffic.

## Strategic Guidelines
1. Avoid high-risk chokepoints directly at stadium gates.
2. Incentivize brand placement along the 400-800m egress corridors.
3. Adapt placement dynamically based on daily urban heat indices.
