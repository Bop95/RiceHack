# Milestone August 3: Score & Recommend
**Project:** World Cup Commerce Without Congestion (Rice Hackathon 2026)  

---
## 1. Opportunity-Risk Matrix Logic
- **Business Opportunity Score:** Derived from spending patterns.
- **Mobility Risk Score:** Derived from urban heat indices and POI geometry constraints.
- **Synthetic Requirements:** Per the Hackathon guide, 30-60 vendor-zone scenarios were synthetically generated (Seed=42) using normal and uniform distributions.

## 2. Five Preliminary Findings
1. **Exit Chokepoint Hazard:** Immediate stadium gates have high revenue potential but unacceptable mobility risk scores.
2. **Staggered Corridor Placement:** Vendor zones positioned 400-800m away absorb crowds perfectly.
3. **Precipitation Sensitivity:** Heavy rain shifts sales to indoor-adjacent fan zones.
4. **Heat Exposure Avoidance:** High urban heat indices decrease customer dwell time.
5. **Controlled Queue Management:** Placement in transition corridors requires controlled lanes.
