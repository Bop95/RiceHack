## 1. Dataset Notes & Schema Technical Checklist (Đức Anh)

## 2. Core Strategic Business Questions (Phương Anh)
1. **Pedestrian Concentration:** How do visit volumes concentrate within 5 miles of MetLife Stadium?
2. **Brand Wallet Share:** Which retail brands capture the highest share of spend?
3. **Weather Vulnerability:** How do precipitation and extreme temperatures impact spend?
4. **Demographics:** What income brackets dominate visitors?
5. **Urban Heat Impact:** How does thermal stress affect dwell time?
