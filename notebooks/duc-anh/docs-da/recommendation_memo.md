# Milestone August 4: Final Recommendation Memo
**Project:** FinalFlow Integration (Rice Hackathon 2026)  
**Target:** World Cup Organizers & FinalFlow Integration Layer

---
## Executive Summary
World Cup organizers must balance commercial brand revenue with spectator safety and transit efficiency. The core finding of this project is that **the best commercial location is not simply the busiest location**, but a zone with strong customer potential that does not obstruct critical transportation flow.

## Key Recommendations
1. **Encourage Purchases in Designated Zones:** Direct commercial activity 400-800m from gate exits.
2. **Enforce Controlled Corridors:** Implement strict queue boundaries in medium-risk areas.
3. **Weather & Thermal Adaptation:** Deploy shaded canopy structures in high heat index zones.
